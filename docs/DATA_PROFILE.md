# PUCAR dataset: data profile

Generated by `scripts/data_profile.py` from `data/pucar/` (as-of date 2026-09-24). Anonymised cheque-dishonour complaints (NI Act s.138) in a Kerala magistrate court under BNSS 2023. Every number below is recomputed on each run.

Lifecycle the data implies: Admission -> Delay Condonation Hearing (only if the complaint is late) -> Cognizance -> Appearance (loops through Warrant when summons are not served or the accused is absent) -> Plea -> Examination u/s 351 BNSS -> Evidence Complainant -> Evidence Accused -> Arguments -> Judgement. Side purposes: Bail, Reports (mediation reports), Application Review.

## Key findings

- The backlog is old: median case age 3.4 years; 30 of 100 cases are 4+ years old and 15 are 5+.
- Cases have already had 2258 hearings between them (median 25, max 44); 37 sit in evidence and 15 are at Judgement.
- Late filing is the norm, not the exception: 96 of 100 roster cases had at least one Delay Condonation hearing (mean 3.33 per case).
- Process is the largest failure pool: 314 of 880 failed hearings (35.7%) are Awaiting Process/Summons/Warrant Return, 196 of them in Warrant alone (Warrant is only 13.5% substantive).
- Absence is second: 195 failures (22.2%). In the last hearing the accused was absent in 84 of 100 cases.
- A full main-flow case costs about 848 judge minutes over 39.9 hearings; at reference gaps that is 1.6 years elapsed, at a flat 60-day gap 6.6 years. Evidence is 46.7% of the minutes.
- The 2026-09-22 list had 90 matters needing 1460 minutes if all were heard (4.42x a 330-minute day), but only 27.7 are expected to be substantive, needing 430 minutes. Q4 2026 has 63 working days (20,790 judge minutes).
- The real day is 39% Appearance; the roster's next purposes are only 5% Appearance (TVD 52 pp), so roster-based lists will skew late-stage.
- 31 of 42 advocates hold 2+ cases (89 cases; max 6): same-day clustering is available. Every party_id is unique, so there is no party clustering.
- Filing-stage checks target real loops: Delay Condonation averages 3.37 hearings/case at 29.3% substantive, and complete accused addresses/phone/email attack the 314 Awaiting Process failures.
